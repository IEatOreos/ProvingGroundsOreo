How to start a match

  1. When Steam asks how to start MCC, choose the option with anti-cheat off.
  2. In MCC open Multiplayer > Custom Multiplayer (offline custom games only).
  3. Select the BOTS tile next to START (or press B) to pick your bots.

First-time setup

Builds the 3D bots, emblems, effects and map pictures from your own copy of the game. Runs once and takes a few minutes.
