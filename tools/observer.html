    On the map

    Dots are players in their team's colour (white ring: a person). The line out of a dot is where it faces; the picked bot also shows its view cone. Goal lines: fight, objective, hunt, retreat, item, hold. Small dots: next waypoint. Hollow circles: where the picked bot believes enemies are (solid ring: seen now). Diamonds: director orders. Speech bubbles: callouts. Flashes: shots. Crosses: deaths. Teal ground: floor LiDAR mapped. You layer: gold squares where you spent the match (brighter: longer), red rings where your fights began.

    Attention (dotted lines ending in a ring): where each player's eyes are. Bots: aiming at an enemy, watching an enemy it knows of (seen, on radar, called out), checking a flank, using equipment, grey: where it walks. People: white, the enemy in their sights. Squads: a thick team-coloured line joins teammates formed up within 8 units. Labels add how long a player has stood still (from 3 s) and how many teammates are with it.

    In the panel, lit chips are what was true when the bot decided; dim chips were false. The list shows everyone's attention, stillness, squad and what each bot is weighing.

    Recording settings

    When a new game starts, the oldest game you did not keep is removed. Kept games, exported games and games with bug reports always stay.

    A longer game keeps its last minutes, so the end of the game and any bug report are always there. Changes count from the next game.
